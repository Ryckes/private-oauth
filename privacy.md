# Privacy Policy — Projects
Personal-use application operated by their owner.

## Data Access

Access to files limited for use by the application.

## Data Storage

Documents owned by the owner of the application stored locally or in Google Drive.

## Data Sharing

The application does not intentionally share scanned documents or Google Drive data with third parties other than the storage services explicitly configured by the owner.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Documents remain under the owner's control and can be deleted from local storage or Google Drive. The owner can revoke Google Drive authorization through their Google Account settings.

## Contact

This application is for personal use and is not publicly distributed.
